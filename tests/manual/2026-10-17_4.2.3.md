## Objective

Verify that a performance captured after normal calibration can be stopped,
downloaded as MIDI, imported into another application, and matched against
MakeShift's captured pitch, velocity, timing and duration data. Distinguish
detection errors from changes introduced by MIDI export.

## Measurements

MIDI format 0, one track, 128 ticks per quarter note, 120 BPM. One tick is
3.90625 ms. Expected start and end ticks are independently rounded from source
milliseconds. Expected velocity is the writer's conversion from 1-100 to
1-127 (`round(sourceVelocity * 127 / 100)`). All values match those conversions.

| Trial | Measurement | Threshold | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cmaj | Pitch/converted velocity/start tick/end tick | Exact match to expected conversion | 15/15 match |
| Cmaj | Maximum absolute start/end rounding differences | At most half one tick: 1.953125 ms | 1.875 / 1.90625 ms |
| Cmaj | Maximum absolute duration difference | At most one tick from independently rounded endpoints: 3.90625 ms | 3.674999997 ms |
| Cmaj | Unclosed notes | Zero | Zero |

These are export quantization checks, not physical onset latency or detection
accuracy measurements. No metronome events were added relative to source data.

## Deviations and Observations

- No intentional chord test was performed at the tester's request. Incidental
  simultaneous notes and existing automated coverage do not establish an
  intentional physical chord performance. Reviewer should assess #107 closure
  against this explicit limitation.
- Record initially remained disabled despite apparent marker detection, then
  enabled without a known intervention. No exact status message was retained.
- C3, D3, E3 and F3 were inaudible or extremely quiet in MakeShift on the laptop
  speakers, even at maximum volume. The MIDI played correctly in REAPER. Source
  velocities for the lower scale notes are smaller than A3/B3/C4; this does not
  establish whether sensing, synthesis or speaker response caused the symptom.
- Low-note audibility and readiness observations are not diagnosed defects or
  physical performance passes. Additional recording after Stop was not tested.
