## Deviations and Observations

Earlier testing exposed clicks only during recording and unstable timing during
other actions. The worklet revision improved timing, but Harry still reported
missing clicks before recording and after Stop. A later approved behavior change
made metronome playback independent of piano readiness and default-off without
toggle persistence. The current retest passed all seven behavior checks.
The tester subsequently confirmed 2cf30d0 as the tested device revision.

Separate observations about declining to save a take and intermittent recording/
Enable audio readiness after calibration were explicitly deferred by the tester.
They are not claimed resolved by this metronome pass.

Harry additionally reported noticing a mismatch between the visual countdown
and audible beats. The size, frequency and triggering conditions were not
measured. The worklet keeps audio timing independent of the page, while visual
updates and the recorder reaction to count-in completion still depend on
main-thread message handling. This is a known limitation for follow-up; visual/
audio synchronization is not claimed verified or fixed. Recording-start
alignment was not separately measured.
