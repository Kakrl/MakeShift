## Preconditions

Production server, calibrated printed sheet and fresh CV tracking; ready audio;
keyboard-style pedal configured to send an assigned code and matching momentary
or tap behavior. Human tester must record exact hardware, browser and commit.

## Test Data

Single notes, ten-note chords, repeated same pitch, captured and later notes,
overlapping sustain/sostenuto, soft on/off during held notes, rapid controls and
simultaneous CV. Compare baseline with pedals enabled under identical conditions.

## Evidence

90 valid permutations of note/sustain/sostenuto press-release pairs, soft-strike,
voice lifetime/reuse, source/UI/storage fixtures and transport overflow reset.
ESLint, TypeScript, contrast (37 pairs) and production build passed locally.
Deployment, keyboard-layout and Chromium audio-browser scripts were attempted;
all failed before execution because the browser could not spawn. No Actions run
evidence exists for this branch yet. User-reported manual functionality evidence follows; specific device compatibility is not established.
