## Deviations and Observations

1. Multiple keys get pressed when trying to press one key. This happens because the model believes other fingers are also interecting with the virtual piano due to all calculatinos being done using the video screen.
2. When pressing quickly, the collision is not guarenteed because that specific frame may not be picked up. This is a framerate issue that stems from latency.
3. I used the piano sheet as the place to press down. This collides with the static virtual piano. For future testing, I should have either a virtual piano that moves with my piano sheet or a taped down piano sheet to stop it from moving.
4. It will be hard to determine if hands are ever resting on the piano sheet or are playing a key. I wonder if this and also the issue of fingers not being used can be circumvented by checking that the finger joint has at least one spot with an angle close to 90 degrees. For example, instead of flat fingers, you have to curl them. Thus, you can ret your fingers by laying them flat.
