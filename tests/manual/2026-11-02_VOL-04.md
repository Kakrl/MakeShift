## Objective

Establish that the maximum is sufficiently loud and usable through speakers and
headphones, with chords and pedal-held notes free of excessive audible distortion.
The initial uniform-gain preview was rejected in user listening feedback on
2026-11-02: soft notes became too loud at high slider settings. The revised
worklet changes velocity math, with maximum multipliers 1.03/1.27/2.08/4 for
velocities 0.1/0.3/0.6/1. Carl Xu subsequently confirmed the revised preview:
"now it sounds good." This is a qualitative listening pass; individual scenarios
and hardware were not specified.

## Preconditions and test data

Use a production build, calibrated live camera and a saved recording containing
soft/loud bass, middle and treble phrases and ten-note chords. Test headphones
and speakers separately at a recorded fixed system volume. Start at low piano
volume and increase gradually. Test both sustain and sostenuto captures live.
