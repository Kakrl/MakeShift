## Objective

Check that the production app loads its runtime assets and pages, plays with
no per-note network requests, and recovers from camera denial and camera loss.
Camera feedback is the first thing a player sees, and deployment problems
would break every session.

Originally recorded in
[tests/README.md](../README.md#deployment-verification-issues-89-and-116);
this report restates that record in the manual template.
