## Objective

Check that the home camera feed stays 16:9, fits inside its wrapper and the
viewport width, and matches the Calibration and About camera sizes on `lg`
screens, in every home state. Before #157 the feed stretched past 16:9 on
wide screens and was smaller than Calibration's.

Originally recorded in
[tests/README.md](../README.md#camera-feed-layout-verification-issue-156);
this report restates that record in the manual template.

  the fold. This was later found by 3.1.1 (#245).
