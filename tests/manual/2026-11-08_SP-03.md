## Test Data

A0–C8 at the same velocity in the sweep, A4 soft and loud, individual notes
held for one second and the ten-note mixed-register chord. The initial
nearest-root sampler changed recordings at MIDI 31, 38, 41, 44, 47, 50, 53,
56, 59, 62, 65, 68, 71, 74, 77, 80, 83 and 91. Compare each with its
immediate lower neighbor on the blended sampler, especially MIDI 30–31.
