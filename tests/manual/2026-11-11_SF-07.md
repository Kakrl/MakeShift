## Objective

Prove that `20261109000000_recording_octaves.sql` applies to a real
database, backfills `octaves` correctly for existing recordings, and that
`GET /api/recordings` returns correct search, sort, octave and `ids` results
against it. Mocked-fetch tests do not cover the SQL or PostgREST behavior.

## Preconditions

- Migrations up to `20261031000000_upload_rate_limits.sql` are applied.
- The database holds at least four recordings with different pitch spans,
  titles and durations, including C4 to C5, C4 to C#5 and B4 to D5, and a
  title containing `%` or `_`.
- Record the pre-migration `octaves`-free state with
  `npx supabase migration list --linked`.
